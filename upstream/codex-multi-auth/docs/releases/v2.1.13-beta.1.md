# v2.1.13-beta.1

Fixes workspace labels vanishing after login, and adds workspace management.

```bash
npm i -g codex-multi-auth@beta
```

## What was wrong

Login captured your account's workspaces and wrote them to disk — and the very next read threw them away. The storage schema didn't declare the `workspaces` and `currentWorkspaceIndex` fields, so validation stripped them on every load.

That's why workspace labels showed up empty after login, and why two accounts on the same email were impossible to tell apart.

## What's fixed

Workspaces now survive a save-and-load round trip, and account labels show the active one:

```
Account 1 ([Personal Plus], user@gmail.com, id:g-AAAA)
```

Accounts without a tracked workspace keep their old label format.

## New

**`status` and `list`** show every workspace under an account that has more than one, marking the active one and flagging disabled ones.

**`workspace <account> [workspace]`** — with just an account index, lists its workspaces; with a workspace index too, switches to it and saves.

**`login --org <org_id>`** binds a login to a specific workspace, so you can register a second one instead of always landing on the default.

## Known issues

- Doesn't fix [#486](https://github.com/ndycode/codex-multi-auth/issues/486) — `doctor` green while the runtime returns `503`.
- Unconfirmed whether a real two-workspace login returns both orgs at once or one at a time. Both paths are supported (`workspace` for the first, `login --org` for the second); a sanitised `accounts.json` from a real two-workspace account would settle it.
